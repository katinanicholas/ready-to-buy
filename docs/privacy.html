What we collect

Your answers to the nine questions, your first name, your email address, whether you checked the optional box for tips and follow-up, and the date and time you submitted. We do not ask for your phone number, and we do not run a credit check.

How we use it

  • To email you your Homebuyer Readiness Report.
  • To let Katina know you completed the assessment so she can be ready if you schedule a call.

We do not sell your information. Your results are educational. They are not a loan application and do not determine mortgage eligibility.

Where it is stored

Submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet in Katina's Google account and sent by Google's email service. If the optional bot check is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile processes limited technical data to tell people from bots. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

How long we keep it

Submission records are deleted automatically after [365] days unless you become a client, in which case records are kept as required for real estate transactions.

Your choices
